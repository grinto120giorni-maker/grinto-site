## Next product research checklist
- Recipe portions and supplier price changes
- Waste by reason and ingredient
- Stock reorder thresholds and unit consistency
- Labor costs (employer costs + payroll)
- Prep production forecasts (require real sales history; no fake predictions)
- HACCP record templates only with country-specific review, not compliance guarantees
- Delivery commissions, packaging and channel profitability
- Menu engineering with clear distinction between contribution margin and net profit

## Safety and trust
Never make compliance guarantees, contact members unsolicited or claim a restaurant has tested a tool without verification.
